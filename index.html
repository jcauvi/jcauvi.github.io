Justine Cauvi

I am a first-year PhD student at DI ENS, working in the ARGO team at INRIA Paris and supervised by Laurent Viennot. Before that, I graduated from École Normale Supérieure de Lyon. You can find more information about me on my resume.

My research interests include graph algorithms, temporal graphs and ordered graphs.
